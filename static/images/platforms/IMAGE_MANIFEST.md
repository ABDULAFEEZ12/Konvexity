# Brand Portfolio & Team Bonding — Image Manifest

All images below are sourced from **Pexels** under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
(free for commercial and personal use, no attribution required, modification permitted). Every image
was inspected directly on its Pexels photo page before download to confirm it carries no watermark and
is not an Unsplash+ / paid-preview asset. Originals were downloaded at full resolution, then resized and
converted to compressed WebP for production use (see "Optimized file" column).

Images not listed here (`arakunrin.jpg`, `konvexity-entrepreneurship.jpg`) are unrelated pre-existing
assets and were not touched in this pass. The five LWB destination photos (`lwb-mauritius.jpg`,
`lwb-ghana.jpg`, `lwb-rwanda.jpg`, `lwb-dubai.jpg`, `lwb-london.jpg`) were sourced and reviewed in an
earlier pass (also from the same source PDF review process, individually checked for watermarks) and
were left in place rather than re-sourced, since they are generic unbranded aerial city photography
matched 1:1 to each named destination — see note at the end of this file.
